Redesign · P01

What RMs see before a meeting at top firms

Two research passes (2026-10-11): vendor and bank sources, NN/G usability research, Carbon design rules. The evidence is mostly vendor material; Morgan Stanley, UBS and J.P. Morgan publish descriptions, not screens. Tab 2 shows the real screens.

Patterns that come back again and again

1. A short meeting brief first. UBS sends an automatic "cheat sheet" before each meeting (portfolio, market moves, talking points). Salesforce Meeting Concierge has a "Client Summary" card with 4 labelled bullets; Microsoft calls it a "client snapshot".
2. Talking points / agenda as their own block. Zocks "Proposed Agenda", Salesforce "talking points", Microsoft Copilot "Highlights" with exactly 3 bullets, then "Show all".
3. The system proposes, the person decides. Morgan Stanley Next Best Action: about 20 ideas a day, the advisor decides what to send (used by more than 90 % of advisors). Salesforce shows Accept / Reject. One solid primary button, in a fixed place.
4. A source next to each fact. Jump and Microsoft show inline source icons or numbered citations; Salesforce has a collapsible "Sources (n)". We have "Kanıt" — keep it small and next to the value.
5. A readiness checklist. Salesforce Meeting Concierge shows "Meeting Prep Readiness 0/10" above the fold; Zocks flags missing data to collect in the meeting. For us this is "Teyit edin" — and the SPK suitability rule (yerindelik testi) makes it a real step before advice.
6. The meeting lifecycle as navigation. Salesforce: Meeting Prep | Meeting Session | Meeting Follow-Up tabs. For us: Hazırlık → Görüşme metni.
7. Long lists: count in the title, 3–5 rows, then "View all". Salesforce, Microsoft. Secondary cards collapsed.
8. Life events and alerts as conversation triggers. Microsoft "Life events" strip; UBS watches liquidity needs and maturing deposits. We do not have this data now (later idea).

What they keep off the first screen

Zocks splits Agenda and Details into two tabs; Salesforce limits the AI summary to two paragraphs. NN/G: show only the few most important things first; more than 2 levels of disclosure hurt; too many alerts make users miss the real ones. For us: assumptions, data notes, eliminated options and the further categories go behind one clearly named "Detaylar" control.

Turkey

ING Türkiye has an internal "Genel Yatırım Öneri Platformu" for investment teams and RMs (Stevie award, August 2026; source); no screens are public. No public RM pre-meeting screens were found for Garanti BBVA, İş Bankası, Akbank or Yapı Kredi (their AI tools face the customer: Ugi, Maxi).

How RMs and advisors actually see the screen

Official images, shown from the vendors' own sites (click a title for the source page). They are the property of those firms; we only learn from them.

Salesforce Agentforce — Meeting Concierge, "Meeting Prep" tab 2026

Header with client and meeting, tabs Meeting Prep | Session | Follow-Up, a full-width readiness bar 0/10, a "Client Summary" card (4 bullets + "Sources (2)"), a task list (5 rows, View All), a numbered "Meeting Prep Brief", one blue primary button "Generate Discussion Guide".
We take: readiness line, client summary with sources, lifecycle tabs, one primary button.

Microsoft 365 Copilot — meeting prep 2026

A tinted Highlights card with exactly 3 bullets and "Show all"; pill tabs Meeting overview | Company overview | Attendees | Talking points; numbered citations; a "Card / All in one" view toggle.
We take: 3 highlights max, then "Tümü"; citations as small numbers.

Microsoft Cloud for Financial Services — Unified customer profile retail banking

Left "Customer snapshot" with segment chips; a full-width Life events strip; one holdings card with sub-tabs; lots of white space, one blue accent.
We take: calm white space, one accent colour, a snapshot column for the client.

Salesforce Financial Services Cloud — Household

A KPI strip of 4 numbers under the name; tabs for the detail; each list shows a few rows, the count in the title ("6+") and "View All".
We take: a KPI strip for the client; counts in titles; "Tümünü gör".

Jump — pre-meeting brief AI for advisors

A document, not a dashboard: "Meeting prep for Client Review", sections Key Updates / Personal Info; an inline source icon on each fact.
We take: the meeting text as a calm document with small source marks.

Zocks — Proposed Agenda

One card, 4 agenda items as checkable circles, "+ Add new agenda".
We take: talking points as a checkable agenda.

Salesforce Agentforce — Plans & Goals

KPI strip, goal cards with priority badge and progress bar, a docked assistant panel.
We take (later): client goals with a priority badge.

Wealthbox — contact marketing image

Client at the centre with tag chips; next meeting; a workflow "Client Review 2/8 steps" with a progress bar.
We take: a step counter for the preparation.

Description only (no public screens)

Morgan Stanley Next Best Action: about 20 ideas a day; the advisor decides (AdvisorHub). Morgan Stanley Debrief: notes, actions and an email draft that the advisor edits (press release). UBS: automatic pre-meeting cheat sheet with talking points (UBS). J.P. Morgan Coach: client preparation support (press only).
