# Found and fixed

A record of the problems we found in the prototype and how we fixed them: what we
saw, how we found the cause (evidence, not a guess), the fix, and the measured
result. For the competition deliverables (the user, 2026-10-17: "the competition
demands something like that"); the final selection for the deck is made at the
end. Newest first. Lead-owned.
